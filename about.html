Who we are

Technology should open doors, not add hurdles.

Lumen Logic is a technology company based in Rundu, Kavango East. We work with businesses to make digital tools more accessible, useful and connected to the way people actually work.

From building your first business website to automating a time-consuming process or finding the right office equipment, we bring a thoughtful, hands-on approach to every conversation.

Our goal is simple: help local businesses move forward with confidence—and be a reliable partner along the way.
